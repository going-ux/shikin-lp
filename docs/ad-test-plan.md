## Proposed budget and stop rules
User has not selected a spend cap. Proposed initial cap: total ¥10,000, ¥1,000/day nominal, manual maximum CPC ¥100 as an initial ceiling, 10 days maximum. These are risk limits, not a forecast; low eligible volume may require revisiting bids without increasing the cap. Daily budgets can overdeliver; use a cost monitor and pause before remaining cap can be exceeded. Do not claim a guaranteed hard cap from a daily budget.

Immediately pause for invalid landing URLs, missing/duplicate measurement, trademark violations, broken affiliate attribution or ineligible routing. At ¥5,000 with no verified applications, pause to review search intent and ASP reporting latency. Stop acquisition by the total cap even while approvals are pending. Wait for actual ASP maturity (30–45 days estimates observed) before judging approved profit; no unapproved reward counted as realized revenue. Scale only with enough matured outcomes and positive contribution, not a single success.

CPA allowable = approved reward minus target contribution and variable costs. Break-even CPC uses measured application rate × measured approval rate × actual reward. Do not insert guessed rates as forecasts.

## Experiment order
Gather baseline first; no simultaneous headline/order/button changes. Then test 2 vs 3 primary providers in the same audience, stable 50/50 allocation and valid ASP sub-ID if permitted. Corporate expansion clicks are secondary. Primary: approved contribution per paid click. Set sample size and minimum meaningful effect from baseline data before allocation; test not active.
